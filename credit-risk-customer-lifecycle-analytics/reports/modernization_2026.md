# 2026 Modernization Layer

This project now includes a governance module that makes the credit-risk story closer to real fintech decisioning:

- Reject-inference proxy for declined-book risk analysis.
- Champion/challenger model verdicts with ROC-AUC and KS guardrails.
- Lifecycle survival segmentation combining churn, margin, and PD.
- Interview-safe caveat: reject inference is a proxy because true outcomes for declined customers are unavailable.

Resume angle: credit risk is no longer only a classifier; it is a policy, lifecycle, fairness, and monitoring system.
